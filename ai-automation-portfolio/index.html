HOW I THINK

I care about what happens after the demo.

I don't want an automation that only looks impressive in a screenshot. I want the workflow to have a clear purpose, reliable data flow, sensible failure handling, and a measurable reason for existing.

That means starting with the business problem, designing the system around it, testing the important paths, and documenting what was built.
